01

Social engineering

Scammers exploit urgency, authority and emotional pressure. A technically secure device does not prevent a user from being manipulated.

02

The usability gap

Existing tools often focus on technical detection or post-incident response. Users need a simple decision aid at the critical moment.

03

Explain, don't scare

Literature reviewed by the team supports human-centred warnings: detect the risk, explain why, then guide the next action.
